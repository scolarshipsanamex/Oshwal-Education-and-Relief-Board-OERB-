Guidelines & Checklist

Eligibility & Required Documents

Applicants must demonstrate genuine financial need and hold an active admission or continuing student status at an accredited tertiary institution.

Mandatory Supporting Documents

  • Academic Admission Letter: Official calling letter or current student ID / fee structure from your University, Medical Training College, or TVET institution.
  • Identification Document: Copy of National ID Card, Passport, or Birth Certificate (for applicants under 18).
  • Academic Transcripts / Results: Secondary school certificate or latest college/university semester transcripts.
  • Financial Need & Vulnerability Verification: Supporting documentation such as parent death certificate(s) for orphans, guardian income statement, or local administrative endorsement.

Independent Review Process

All applications are evaluated strictly on verified financial vulnerability and academic commitment by an independent Grant Selection Committee. Submission of an application initiates verification and does not guarantee an award until committee vetting is concluded. All applications submitted without pay shall be automatically be disqualified.
